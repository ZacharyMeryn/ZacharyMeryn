## Zach Meryn

Hi all, I'm Zach Meryn, a Mechanical Engineering and Mathematics major at Vanderbilt University (BE/MS, 2029).

I work on control theory for robotic systems, currently in the ARMA Lab under
Dr. Nabil Simaan, where my projects are a cable-driven parallel robot testbed
and a continuum robot for minimally invasive colonoscopy procedures.  


**Research interests:** cable-driven parallel robots, continuum robotics,  
fuzzy logic control, control theory, nonlinear control, kinematics  

**Selected work**

  (book chapter, in review, Studies in Fuzziness and Soft Computing series)  
- Reconfigurable Enhanced Spike-Event Neuromorphic Detection of Quasi-DC Signals in Noisy Environments (IEEE, co-author)
